Roasting Your Friends at the Hazari Table: Funny Leaderboard Tags

Hazari is a game of banter. How funny leaderboard tags work, example lines in English and Bangla, and how to roast without hurting anyone.

Tags

  • Hazari
  • Card Games
  • Banter
  • Bangla

Published November 24, 2026.

A good Hazari night is half cards and half teasing. Whoever leads gets a boast, whoever trails gets a roast, and everyone laughs. I built that into the scorekeeper's leaderboard, so here is how it works, with a few lines you can steal.

How the tags work

  • Each player gets a short tag based on where they stand on the leaderboard: first, second, third or last.
  • Tags only appear once two rounds have been saved, so the early scores are not teased.
  • The tag stays the same until the next round is saved, so it does not flicker while you look at it.
  • Bangla players get Bangla tags and English players get English ones, so the joke lands in the language you play in.

A few English examples

  • First place: “Boss mode”, “Main character”, “Chef mode: cooked everyone”.
  • Second place: “Breathing down their neck”, “Silver medal, smiling anyway”, “Eternal runner-up”.
  • Third place: “Mid and proud”, “Stuck in traffic”, “Background dancer energy”.
  • Last place: “Donating points”, “Cards hate me today”.

And in Bangla

  • প্রথম: “রাজা এসে গেছে, বস মোড অন”।
  • দ্বিতীয়: “ঘাড়ের কাছে নিঃশ্বাস”।
  • শেষ: “দান-খয়রাত চলছে…”।

How to roast kindly

  1. Tease the position, not the person. “Donating points” is about the score. Jokes about someone's looks, family or background are not.
  2. Share the loser's seat. Everybody ends up last at some point, so the same joke is aimed at the whole table over an evening.
  3. Keep it short. A good tease is one line.
  4. Stop when someone is not laughing. If a joke lands badly, drop it.

The tags are playful by design, and they are for friends who already like each other. If your table prefers a quieter game, play without showing the leaderboard.

Tags suit a long game, because they only appear after two rounds and change with every saved round. See how many deals to expect. For the Bangla side of the app, read about the card names and how Bangla is handled.

Use them

Keep score in the scorekeeper, and the tags appear on its leaderboard from the second round. Share the live scoreboard and your friends can watch the roasts update. The guide to sharing a live scoreboard shows how.
