Let Friends Watch Your Hazari Game Live: How to Share a Scoreboard

How to share a live, read-only Hazari scoreboard by link so friends and family who are not at the table can follow every round.

Tags

  • Hazari
  • Card Games
  • Live Scoreboard
  • Sharing

Published November 24, 2026.

Some of the best fans of a Hazari game are not at the table. A cousin abroad, a friend who is running late, the family in the next room. A read-only live scoreboard lets them all follow the game from their own phones.

How to share

  1. One person at the table keeps score in the scorekeeper.
  2. Open the game, choose Share live scoreboard, then Start sharing.
  3. Send the link, or let people scan the QR code on your screen.
  4. Everyone who opens it sees the leaderboard update as each round is saved.

You do not need an account to start. Sharing starts as a guest by default, and signing in with Google is optional.

What viewers see

  • The ranked leaderboard, with who is leading and who deals next.
  • A table of the rounds so far.
  • Small highlights, such as a winning streak, and the funny tags that go with the standings.

Viewers cannot change a score, and they do not see the scorer's drafts, other games or sign-in details. The scorer's phone stays the single source of truth.

One link for the whole evening

When a game ends and you press Play again, the link stays the same, so friends keep watching without a new link. Viewers follow the current game automatically and can look back at earlier games from the same session.

Limits worth knowing

  • The scorer's browser has to stay open while the game is running, because it is the scorer's phone that sends the updates.
  • Shared links expire on their own, and the scorer can stop sharing at any time. Stopping closes the link.
  • Scoring itself works without a connection. Only the live view needs the internet.

If you want the leaders teased while everyone watches, read about funny leaderboard tags. If you are still deciding how to keep score, paper or an app? compares the options.

A short guide to sharing a live scoreboard is on the app's site, and the design of the live scoreboard is explained here for the curious.

Play it or score it

Play Hazari alone against the computer, or keep score and share the banter with your friends. It is free and works offline.
