Research

My research has focused on language understanding for Bengali and deep learning for ECG analysis.

Published paper

2023 · ICCIT · IEEE Xplore

Bengali Intent Classification with Generative Adversarial BERT — co-authored with Mohammad Jahid Ibna Basher and Md. Tanvir Rouf Shawon. The work introduces BNIntent30, a Bengali intent-classification dataset with 30 classes, and evaluates GAN-BnBERT against other classification approaches on that dataset.

Undergraduate thesis
